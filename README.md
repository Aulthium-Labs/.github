# 🧪 Aulthium LABS

> **Exploring what AI can do.**

Aulthium LABS is an experimental organization focused on exploring the capabilities, limitations, and possibilities of AI through software.

Currently, Aulthium LABS has one project:

```text
Aulthium LABS
└── Aulthium CLI
```

## 🚀 Aulthium CLI

**Aulthium CLI** is the first project under Aulthium LABS.

It is an experimental command-line AI environment built to explore what AI can do when given access to a terminal, tools, and real-world workflows.

The goal isn't simply to build another AI chatbot.

The goal is to **experiment with the boundaries of AI**.

```text
AI
├── What can it do?
├── What can it automate?
├── What does it struggle with?
├── Where does it reach its limits?
└── What happens when we give it more tools?
```

## 🔬 What We Explore

Aulthium CLI may be used to experiment with:

- AI agents and autonomous workflows
- Terminal interaction
- Tool usage
- Different AI models
- Different AI providers
- BYOK configurations
- Context and memory
- Automation
- AI limitations and failure cases
- Human + AI workflows
- New approaches to AI-assisted development

There is no requirement for every experiment to use the same model, provider, or architecture.

**The experiment comes first.**

## 🔑 AI Provider Flexibility

Aulthium CLI **can support BYOK**, but BYOK is not the defining purpose of Aulthium.

Depending on the experiment, Aulthium CLI may work with different models, providers, or configurations.

```text
Aulthium CLI
     │
     ├── Different AI models
     ├── Different AI providers
     ├── BYOK
     └── Future experiments
```

The purpose is to discover what different approaches can do rather than lock the project to one particular AI system.

## 🧠 The Core Question

Aulthium LABS is interested in two simple questions:

> **What can AI actually do?**

and

> **What can't it do?**

Understanding limitations is just as valuable as discovering new capabilities.

An experiment that fails can still be useful if it teaches us something.

## 🧪 Experimental by Design

Aulthium projects may change rapidly.

Experiments can be:

```text
Idea
 ↓
Experiment
 ↓
Test
 ↓
Observe
 ↓
Learn
 ↓
Improve / Replace / Remove
```

Not every experiment is expected to become a permanent feature.
